insurance agents: purchase receipts, parcel shipments, and event expenses).

Rules for this run:

- Do NOT click "데모 종료" (demo exit) until your session is over — it wipes

Explore as a first-time user, in this order:

2. Evidence list (자료 tab): try the type filter and the year filter.

Look for: cut-off text, hidden or overlapping buttons, buttons that do
nothing, the keyboard covering inputs, and anything that blocks the main
flow. A blocked step is a finding, not a reason to stop: record it and
continue with the next item. Budget about 30 actions, screenshot after
every action.
